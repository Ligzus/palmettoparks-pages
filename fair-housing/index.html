Fair Housing & Accessibility

Equal Housing Opportunity

Palmetto Parks complies with the federal Fair Housing Act and South Carolina Fair Housing Law. We do not discriminate because of race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status (including pregnancy or children under 18), national origin, or any other characteristic protected by applicable law. This applies to home rentals, applications, and the terms, conditions and services offered to residents.

Requesting a reasonable accommodation or modification

If you or a member of your household has a disability, you can request:

  • A reasonable accommodation — a change in a rule, policy, practice or service. Examples include assistance with an application, an alternative format, a reserved parking space, extra time to provide documents, or an assistance animal where pets are otherwise restricted.
  • A reasonable modification — a physical change to a home or shared area, such as a ramp, grab bars or a lever-style door handle.

You may make your request before or after applying or moving in, in person or by phone, text or email. No special wording or form is required.

  • Call or text 843-882-7042. Callers who are deaf, hard of hearing or have a speech disability can use South Carolina Relay by dialing 711.
  • Email: manager@palmettoparks.com
  • Mail: Palmetto Parks Holdings LLC, 295 Seven Farms Drive, Suite C-297, Daniel Island, SC 29492

We will reply promptly and may discuss the request with you. Where the disability-related need is not apparent, we may request reliable information supporting that need. We will not request a diagnosis or medical records.

Help with our rental application

We can read the rental application (PDF, 3 pages) aloud, provide it in large print, or help you complete it by phone or in person. Use any of the contact methods above to request assistance.

Accessibility Statement

Palmetto Parks wants people with disabilities to have access to this website and to applications for our homes. Our aim is to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. We review accessibility when updating the site.

If you experience difficulty using a page or need photographs, property information, the application or other website material in a different format, contact us for help:

  • Call or text 843-882-7042 (TTY/relay users can dial 711 for South Carolina Relay)
  • Email manager@palmettoparks.com

Include the page address and a description of the problem so that we can assist you promptly.

If you believe you have experienced discrimination

You can contact the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) by calling 1-800-669-9777 or visiting hud.gov/fairhousing. The South Carolina Human Affairs Commission is available at schac.sc.gov.
